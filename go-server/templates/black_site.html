{{icon "shield-halved" "me-1"}} Threat Classification

APT Advanced Persistent Threat. Systemic. Architectural. This bug corrupts the entire user experience and will be found by anyone who looks carefully. Fix before anyone sees this in public.
Zero-Day Unpatched Exploit. Actively exploitable, high-impact, no workaround. Users are hitting this right now and we have no defense.
Exploit Known Vulnerability. Documented weakness being used against us. We know it’s there, we know the damage, we haven’t patched it yet.
CVE Common Vulnerability. Design debt with a known attack surface. Not actively exploited but attackable. Track and remediate systematically.
IOC Indicator of Compromise. Evidence that something isn’t right. Minor, but left unaddressed it metastasizes.
